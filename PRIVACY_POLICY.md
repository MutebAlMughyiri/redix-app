# Privacy policy

REDIX's privacy policy, in Arabic and English, is at **[https://redix.muteb.net:8443/privacy](https://redix.muteb.net:8443/privacy)**:
what REDIX keeps about a person, why, where, for how long, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be removed.

It has one copy, which the API serves, so the page and what the stores link to never differ. Its source is
`go-backend/internal/legal/privacy.html` in redix-api.
